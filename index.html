Which app fits your child’s curiosity?

For picture-puzzle explorers

Puzzle Garden is for children ages 3–7 who enjoy fitting pieces together to reveal a picture. Start with fewer pieces, then choose a larger puzzle when your child wants a different challenge.

Try the free monkey puzzle together in your browser before exploring the app.

For animal-sound explorers

Animal Sounds gives parents and little learners a way to explore animal voices, pictures, puzzles, and quizzes together. Listen to a sound, name the animal, and talk about what you hear.

Try six animal sounds in your browser to see what catches your child’s interest.

Questions from parents

Which devices can we use?

Both Puzzle Garden and Animal Sounds are available for iPhone and iPad through the App Store, and for Android through Google Play. Check each store listing for current device and operating-system requirements.

Which app should we try first?

Choose Puzzle Garden for picture puzzles with different piece counts, or Animal Sounds for listening to animals and exploring pictures and games together. Both app websites have browser demos linked above.

Where can I find current prices and purchases?

Use the App Store and Google Play buttons for each app above. Those listings show current prices and available in-app purchases for your region. Animal Sounds includes a free package of 12 animals, with optional purchases for additional content.

Can Puzzle Garden be played offline?

Yes. Puzzle Garden bundles its puzzle pictures for offline play. Download the app before taking it on a trip. The browser demos need an internet connection to load.

Where can I read the apps’ privacy policies?

Read the Puzzle Garden privacy policy and the Animal Sounds privacy policy for app-specific information. The Kostin Apps website privacy policy explains analytics on this website.
